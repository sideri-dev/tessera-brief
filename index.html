Last dossier

The Bouroullec brothers' "split" studio was never dissolved — the joint company remains active with both brothers as co-managers, while each brother's personal art-side practice has run as a separate sole-trader entity since before the public 2024 split, making the design/art boundary first a legal-entity boundary. Every counterparty draws that line differently: the practice avoids "art" for its gallery work, the gallery is first to call him an "artist," manufacturers freeze the duo credit on the old catalogue and start a solo credit with each new product code, and museums file nearly everything as design.

Inside the gallery's own pricing the art-labelled drawings and vases are the cheap end, far below furniture and lighting — the highest tier is a bespoke commission, the exact route a spatial practice already sells.
