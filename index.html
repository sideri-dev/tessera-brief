AICA's new ジョリパット topcoat is explicitly excluded from 不燃 certification.

AICA's new mica-flecked ジョリパット グラノバ topcoat launched August 21, 2026, in 21 colors across 67 compatible patterns, priced at the base Jolipad pattern price plus a fee of ¥2,700/㎡ (設計価格, 消費税抜き, on a 300㎡ material-and-labor basis), with an annual sales target of ¥50,000,000 or more.

It carries an unambiguous exemption stated verbatim on AICA's own release — "不燃認定対象外です" — so it can only go on feature walls or other non-rated surfaces, and anything it touches needs a certified substrate elsewhere on the same schedule. Worth stating up front, before it becomes a surprise at plan-check.

DAIKEN's new "Artio" flooring prints directly onto substrate — no F☆☆☆☆ rating stated.

DAIKEN's new Artio flooring prints its pattern directly onto substrate instead of laminating a veneer or sheet facing, a first for the category by the manufacturer's own account, in 303×1,818mm, 12mm-thick planks on a special MDF/eco-plywood base. It costs ¥50,500/box (tax excl.) for the 2P pattern or ¥54,900/box for other patterns, backed by roughly ¥800 million in dedicated production capacity and a 300,000m²/year target by FY2029.

Neither DAIKEN's release nor the independent trade coverage states an F☆☆☆☆ formaldehyde rating — treat it as unconfirmed for enclosed interior use until that turns up.

Capella Kyoto's courtyard exists because the site was a former elementary school.

Capella Kyoto, open since March 2026 with 89 rooms, sits on the former grounds of 新道小学校, and Kengo Kuma has explained the courtyard massing as a direct response: placing a "foreign object" on a site with that community history felt wrong, so the design opens outward under a 唐破風 roof rather than enclosing the block. Brewin Design Office did the interiors — lacquer, washi, ceramic, 檜/杉/竹/錦織.

A usable, quotable precedent for any brief touching a heritage-adjacent or community-memory site — the move is an open gesture, not concealment.

Time & Style's 2026 collection names four separate 産地 down to the joinery.

Time & Style's 2026 collection names its provenance piece by piece: the Grid composition storage system is solid Hokkaido oak joined with dovetail (アリ継ぎ) work; the Yoshino Breath Armchair is made entirely from Yoshino cedar using traditional technique; the Amakakeru light pairs Mino washi with 指物 joinery; and the Nagare/Tokio pieces, made with OEO Studio, are hand-cast by craftsmen in Toyama prefecture.

Sanseido's Kanda-Jimbocho flagship solves a shelf-as-handrail conflict with a code-driven fix.

Sanseido's flagship encodes its shelving into a rule: shelves on levels 1–5 get angled bases, and level 6 and above sit flat at roughly eye height, about 1,600mm. The sharper decision is where a shelf couldn't double as a required handrail near a mini-staircase — architect Go Hasegawa's office built a separate wall topped with a rail styled after the bookshelf's own side-panel profile, instead of dropping in a generic code handrail that would have broken the design language.

A concrete answer to a fitout conflict that comes up constantly in retail work. The design-mechanism detail above is single-sourced.

Last dossier

令和6年告示第8号 is a 努力義務 benchmark, not a tariff — it prescribes hours times rate times 2.1, and at the ministry's own reference rate produces roughly ¥45.6M tax-excl. for full design-plus-supervision of a 1,000㎡ hotel. For interior and art-direction work specifically it's silent in three places that matter: interior design and FF&E selection count as 追加的業務 with no standard hours, renovation outside 耐震改修 sits formally outside the table, and non-建築士 interior practices sit outside its scope entirely — which makes the tables a defensible floor argument, not a rate card.

Most hospitality-adjacent base tables (restaurant 商業第1類/第2類, banquet-hotel 宿泊第2類) were left unrevised for lack of survey data, so the money in the 2024 revision sits in stacking coefficients and the annually escalating reference 単価 (+4.3% this year, 14th straight rise) — while the market has already repriced on its own (74% of surveyed firms raised fees in three years, per 日経). The defunct predecessor, 告示第98号, survives mainly in printed contract templates and stale municipal pages, not in any operative post-2024 積算基準 — so a brief still citing it is a template-inheritance error with a precise fix, not a grounded pricing choice.
