A Sapporo concert-hall renovation shows exactly what an ECI technical-cooperation fee buys, and it isn't construction.

Sapporo's second-phase renovation of its 28-year-old concert hall (Kitara) is being let via ECI — the contractor competes on a construction-cost ratio against a disclaimed 参考額 (not a 予定価格), while a separate, small, non-price-competed technical-cooperation fee of ¥46,063,600 against an ¥8,648,000,000 construction reference buys the winning contractor a seat at a formal four-party 協議会 with the client and two designers during 実施設計 — before any construction contract exists.

The eligibility gate reuses 令和6年告示第8号's own facility-type taxonomy (第1類/第2類 文化・交流・公益施設, ≥10,000㎡) as a bidder qualification filter rather than a fee-calculation input, which is a genuinely new use of that standard. One correction carried over from the source document: the named equipment designer is (株)ビーゴーイング, not the "ピーゴーイング" first circulated.

LIXIL is selling 100%-recycled aluminum as an extruded profile to an outside hardware maker for the first time.

A named fabricator (株式会社アシスト, Osaka) will sell stair-nosing and floor-trim hardware made from LIXIL's "PremiAL R100" recycled aluminum from November 2026 — LIXIL's own release calls this the first time it has supplied PremiAL R100 as a profile to a third-party maker, and claims an 80% CO2 cut versus virgin ingot (LIXIL's own estimate, not independently verified).

Last dossier

The oldest open thread in the practice lane — whether Japan's 2004 commitment to publish 落札率 data for consulting contracts was ever honoured — turns out to have been absorbed rather than dropped: a 2006 Finance Ministry circular (財計第2017号, amended 2026-04-01) makes a mandatory monthly 落札率 table a standing requirement for every national contract above ¥2,000,000 (予決令第99条第7号), not the higher threshold the corpus had been assuming. That also explains why no dataset splitting 落札率 by price-competition weight had surfaced: the relevant columns sit side by side on every national case-level register, but the published aggregate statistics only require counts and totals — the split exists in every register and in no summary. A prior reading — that quality-weighted bidding (総合評価) clears above plain price competition in Miyazaki — is withdrawn: restricted to its one true business category, it actually clears 5–14 points below price-only competition in all three fully verified editions. The cleanest real dataset, from the central government's own FY2026 facilities-bureau figures, orders 落札率 monotonically by price weight, and retires two drifting inferences: the ~80% clustering in price-scored civil design is bidders hitting the floor price to the yen, and a high 落札率 is not itself evidence of a non-price route.
