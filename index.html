Today

A Shinwa Auction sale just showed exactly how wide the estimate-to-hammer spread can run on decorative lots.

The 10/17 Western-art/jewelry sale cleared ¥160,223,750 hammer-plus-premium at 72.5% clearance, and two lots blew past estimate by an order of magnitude: a painted porcelain plaque estimated at ¥15–25万 sold for ¥1,207万5,000 (roughly 48–80x), and a jade obi clasp estimated at ¥10–20万 sold for ¥1,322万5,000 (roughly 66–132x) — against a porcelain urn that cleared inside its ¥200–300万 estimate as the contrast case. The house's own page disambiguates the figure as hammer-plus-premium, not hammer alone. The spread, not the estimate, is the budgeting signal for this catalogue.

OMO7 Yokohama just won a Good Design Gold Award with its full renovation scope-split named and jury-defensible.

星野リゾート's conversion of Murano Togo's 1959 former Yokohama City Hall wing (276 rooms, opened April) reframes the "new-meets-old" brief as an interior concept called "接木" (grafting); credits split cleanly between 竹中工務店 (architecture/construction), 成瀬・猪熊建築設計事務所 (FOH interior supervision), and アイリスオーヤマ/I.P.M (FOH furniture implementation), plus named signage, textile and tile-art credits. The jury's own language — "it feels as though a hotel had always stood here" — is citable in a client room. A usable precedent for structuring and pricing a heritage-shell renovation commission.

A small Hachioji office fit-out just published its entire material and furniture spec down to the SKU.

雨宮知彦's 180.44㎡ renovation for 京王不動産 splits one floor into four striped zones, leaving the skeleton exposed everywhere to hold ceiling height except the communication zone, which gets its own zinc-coated corrugated-steel-clad ceiling for intimacy. Every finish and SKU is named: 竹村工業 cement-wood board, two named サンゲツ carpet-tile SKUs, NORMAN JAPAN wood blinds, ITOKI desking, KOKUYO meeting furniture, named オーデリック line lighting. Directly usable as a cost/spec comparator, and a defensible "buy ceiling height by exposing structure, spend on intimacy only where it matters" argument.

Nagano just added two named makers to its prefectural craft list.

高遠焼 (maker: 高遠焼振興会, Ina City) and 松本お神酒の口 (maker: 矢澤商店, Matsumoto) were designated 知事指定伝統的工芸品 on 2026-09-16, bringing the prefecture's own list to 26 items — a prefectural track, smaller and distinct from METI's national designation. Gives a defensible, dated provenance line for sourcing Nagano ceramics or ceremonial bamboo work, provided it's cited as prefectural rather than national.

A Kinosaki Onsen restaurant rebuild just named the finish that makes the counter the room's focal point.

ninkipen! rebuilt a ~60-year-old, cliff-buried house in Kinosaki Onsen (48.49㎡) — damaged frame replaced, roof re-tiled — and built the counter from cedar board dyed black with iron mordant (鉄媒染), against mortar trowel-finish flooring and plaster walls. A named finishing technique and a disclosed decay-to-repair sequence, usable wherever an existing frame can be salvaged rather than rebuilt from scratch.

Last dossier

The most recent dossier tracks whether Japan's public-works costing standard has actually started moving interior finishes off its 市場単価 (market-rate) basis and onto the priced 歩掛り (labour-coefficient) tables — the shift that would make renovation finishes costable the way new-build already is. As of this run, no interior line has moved: plaster, paint, and the interior board/floor set are still priced by market survey, and the June 2026 update that looked like a precursor turns out to be a pure formatting rewrite that changed no quantity and added no line. Two free, dated public trackers were identified that will register the real migration before the ministry's own press release does.
