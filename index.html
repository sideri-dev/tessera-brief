A Morioka public design-commission contract shows the moral-rights waiver clause to watch for.

盛岡市's revised design-commission contract, effective April 1, 2026, assigns copyright to the contractor alone or jointly with the client (Article 7), caps the client's build-right at one structure plus maintenance-driven modification — 増築・改築・修繕・模様替・維持・管理・運営 (Article 8) — and in Article 9 has the architect waive their moral rights under 著作権法第19条第1項 and 第20条第1項: no name on the building, no publicizing the work, without the client's prior consent, while the client gets free publicity rights over the design.

It's a live example of the clause to read and negotiate separately before signing a Japanese public-sector commission where attribution has commercial value — "the work is public record" doesn't mean the designer's name attaches by default. Worth comparing this municipal form against the 四会連合協定 national standard contract and other municipalities' equivalents.

MLIT will now let 3D-printed mortar formwork count as ordinary RC construction, under a size cap.

A June 15, 2026 MLIT notification lets "積層造形型枠一体型壁式鉄筋コンクリート造" — 3D-printed mortar formwork with rebar and poured concrete — count as standard load-bearing-wall RC for single-storey buildings up to 200m² with a story height (corrected here from an earlier "eave height" draft) of 3.5m or less, removing the prior per-project 建築基準法38条 approval. It covers load-bearing walls, foundation beams, floor and roof slabs and wall beams, not columns — real for a small pavilion or accessory structure on a hospitality site.

Named fabricator Polyuse reports 300+ installations completed and 40 "Polyuse One" units deployed, with 100 planned nationwide this fiscal year; its claimed 90%+ market share is a December 2024 survey figure riding inside the June 2026 release, not current. Confirmed on only one JA industry source this cycle — government-page corroboration wasn't found, a gap worth a retry.

The Iwata family tea-utensil collection cleared ¥2.38 billion hammer-plus-premium at Mainichi Auction.

Mainichi Auction's own February 2026 results (Tokyo, Ariake) put the total at roughly ¥2.38 billion, explicitly labelled hammer-plus-premium, across 306 lots, all sold. The top lot, a Momoyama-era Raku Chōjirō black tea bowl, made ¥920,000,000; a 大名物 Ido tea bowl made ¥310,500,000; a 大名物 tea caddy made ¥98,900,000; a 寧窯 celadon vase made ¥95,450,000 — all hammer-plus-premium.

As a dated, fully-typed ceiling reference for what "important" 茶道具 and 古美術 clear at in a disclosed single-provenance sale, it calibrates a client's expectations between museum-tier and decorative-tier procurement — treat it as a six-month-old historical datapoint, not a current-value claim.

Royal Park Hotel Iconic Naha sits inside a rebuilt bank headquarters tower — ZEB Oriented, base-isolated.

The Bank of the Ryukyus' new 13-storey, 67.5m headquarters tower (40,562㎡, reinforced-concrete with mid-storey base isolation, ZEB Oriented certified) carries a 257-room hotel across floors 7–13, with lobby, front desk, guest-only pool, gym and executive lounge concentrated on the 7th floor — confirmed independently by both the bank's own release and separate trade coverage.

It's a corroborated precedent for inserting hospitality into the upper floors of an existing commercial tower, with the ZEB certification and base isolation disclosed rather than inferred.

Risonare Shimonoseki's waterfront envelope is a 3-metre membrane eave running the full 300-metre perimeter.

Hoshino Resorts' first ground-up Risonare property, opened December 2025, is a 12-storey, roughly 19,181㎡ building with 187 ocean-view rooms. The eave projects about 3 metres from the exterior wall in membrane material around the full ~300-metre perimeter; the walls are exposed-finish plastered concrete, with coloured sections in extruded cement-board (ECP) panels — a named, buildable detail translating a curvilinear, contextual design reading into a specifiable waterfront envelope.

Named team: architect of record Nihon Sekkei (chief architect Yuzuru Tsukagawa), KDa on interior/brand supervision, Toda Corporation as contractor. Single primary trade-press source this cycle, not yet corroborated by a second outlet.

What is measurable is a structural spread between published tariffs at each rung: roughly 10% round-trip at a 骨董 exchange, about 15% of 成立価格 at 東京美術倶楽部, and 27.5–38.5% round-trip at a Japanese auction house, widening as price falls, across exactly the band a hospitality object budget sits in.
