Fukuyama's own basic plan for a 道の駅 rebuild publishes the fee ratio most 設計事務所 never get to see named.

Fukuyama City's March 2026 basic plan for rebuilding 道の駅アリストぬまくま sets combined design and construction-supervision fees at about ¥65 million against a roughly ¥1.64 billion all-in rebuild (¥1.33bn construction, ¥160m exterior works, ¥85m demolition) for a 1,729m² new building — a combined design+supervision fee ratio of about 4.0% of total project cost, no 税込/税抜 basis stated. Worth holding as a comparator against what 告示第8号's 略算法 would produce at this scale — Fukuyama is also one of only ten municipalities nationwide picked for MLIT's 道の駅 "Stage 3" package, the sole Chūgoku-Shikoku selection.

Japan's flame-retardant-textile body is changing how futon and bedding actually get tested for 防炎 compliance.

日本防炎協会 is replacing the 45° meshenamine ignition test with a 45° air-mix-burner wire-mesh method for 寝具等完成品側地 and completed ふとん, effective July 1, 2026 — the meshenamine flame position proved too unstable on polyester-filled futon to give reproducible char-length results. The pass/fail bar is unchanged (max 10.0cm, average 8.0cm), so nothing already certified needs recertifying, but any new bedding/futon 防炎 submission after July 1 tests under the new method.

Toyama's own craft-revival planning document puts 庄川挽物木地 craftspeople at nine, nearly all over 70.

富山県's KOGEIミライ会議 materials — the FY2025–2027 revised programme, not a stale 2022 copy — show 庄川挽物木地 (lathe-turned lacquerware blanks) output falling from roughly ¥700m to about ¥100m, workshops down from 38 to 10, workers down from 136 to 12, and narrower still: the roughly 70 craftspeople working in the 1980s are down to 9, nearly all over 70. A materials-level chokepoint upstream of lacquerware supply generally — worth weighing as lead-time/capacity risk on any commissioned piece planned years out, since the 産地 may simply not be there by delivery.

Singapore's URA names three distinct ways it actually commissions design consultants, each with built examples.

URA's own urban-design page lays out three mechanisms: QFM, a transparent tender weighing fee and design quality, used for most government design-consultancy tenders (NS Square cited); CPT, a two-envelope process shortlisting design concepts before price is reviewed (Fullerton Heritage, Capitol Theatre, One Holland Village); and DAP, an independent design-advisory panel reviewing major proposals (ION Orchard, Marina Barrage, the State Courts Towers). A named menu of commissioning routes for any Singapore-based public design pitch.

Last dossier

The backlog dossier closing out the urethane-foam certification question finds there is no checkpoint at all for the 「適切に被覆」(adequately covered) condition that keeps sprayed urethane/isocyanurate foam legally a 準不燃 material — the rule was born in 2024 as a trade against a model-box fire test, and the 2026 successor certificates fold the covering requirement into the certified spec itself, so the foam is only the certified material when it's actually installed covered. Nothing in 確認申請, 中間検査 or 完了検査 checks that fact in any of the five 特定行政庁 examined (Tokyo, Hiroshima, Kyoto, Nagoya, Kobe) — Tokyo interior renovation gets no 中間検査 at all — and the content of 「適切に」 itself is left to each manufacturer's own installation method, undefined in the law, the Order, or MLIT's guideline. Enforcement is therefore retrospective: a departure surfaces only as a 建築基準法 violation against the 設計者 after the fact, with no body currently positioned to rule otherwise. The dossier also corrects its own parent: the 45 superseded urethane certificate numbers are not legally revoked — the register revokes only 3 of the underlying 52 bases, and the statute cited for revocation power doesn't actually carry one.
