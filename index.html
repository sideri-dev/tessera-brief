Nara Hotel's 1984 west wing reopens with rooms cut from 65 to 52, and salvaged brass fittings turn out to be display pieces, not hardware.

JR West Hotels' nine-month renovation reopened September 2, cutting room count from 65 to 52 to enlarge units — Suite at 67.5㎡, Premium at 44.9–63.9㎡, Superior at 33.7㎡, Standard at 22.3㎡. Guestroom art draws on three named 産地 crafts — 奈良墨 ink, 吉野 hand-made washi, 赤膚焼 ceramics — plus a 正倉院文様 banquet-hall carpet.

Correction worth carrying: the reclaimed 1909 brick and salvaged brass fittings aren't reused as functional hardware, as first reported — they're framed as historical display pieces in the elevator hall, a display-fabrication brief rather than a salvage-as-hardware one. A dated room-count-to-area ratio (roughly 20% fewer keys, by category) worth citing on any renovation pitch.

Hoshinoya Nara Prison opens in a converted Meiji-era prison, with the architect explaining her seismic-bracing choices in her own words.

The 48-suite hotel (opened June 25) combines nine to eleven former ~5㎡ cells into each guestroom. Azuma Rie of 東環境・建築研究所 describes deliberately keeping the steel bracing against the load-bearing brick from looking crude, stripping whitewash to expose original brick, and retaining the vault ceiling — a citable design language for visible seismic retrofit inside a protected shell.

Heritage restoration ran under 安井建築設計事務所, seismic design under 飯島建築事務所. The cell-count-to-area ratio is a transferable planning number for small-cell heritage conversions.

輪島塗 production is still running at 60–70% of pre-earthquake capacity, two and a half years after the 2024 Noto quake — and there's no relocation workaround.

輪島漆器商工業協同組合 confirms capacity hasn't recovered past six to seven-tenths of pre-quake levels, and is calling for産地-wide business-continuity planning given the structural disaster vulnerability. Critically, there's no way around it: 輪島塗's traditional-crafts designation requires production within Wajima city itself, so work made elsewhere can't be sold under the name.

Any commission specifying 輪島塗 needs to budget longer lead times and treat named-craftsperson availability as unverified until confirmed — a durable, 産地-wide constraint, not a single workshop's problem. Last dossier

Art procurement for hospitality — how the budget is actually set (2026-09-02). Only one published document anywhere prices hospitality art as a line item, and read against its own earlier edition it splits into contradictory findings: guestroom art costs roughly doubled since 2017 while overall renovation scope rose only modestly, but lobby and restaurant art's share of budget stays flat on a matched basis.

Japan has no equivalent art-budget line at all — a hospitality art allowance has to be created from scratch, not negotiated against a published norm. Contracts reviewed show operators, not owners, typically hold art-selection authority, and route in-house design labour and purchasing markup through the FF&E reserve rather than operating expenses — keeping both off the line that drives incentive fees.
